P4 · Motor finance — the dropped beat that barked

The pattern was vindicated: motor finance fell silent 15–22 Jun, then barked on 23 Jun with the FCA’s £9bn redress story. But the keyword classifier tagged 4 stories as motor finance — and only one was real. That single false positive is the whole argument for the bounded LLM in Station 4.

This is precisely the judgement a bounded LLM is for, and the back-test handed us its acceptance test: re-run P4, watch the three phantoms drop out, and confirm the 23 Jun bark survives.
