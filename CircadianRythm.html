Movement I · The Four Phases

The Circadian Rhythm of the Archive

Between the three structural stores of the Archive (Corpus → Signal Ledger → Prediction Ledger) runs a continuous metabolic lifecycle. Each phase of the day governs how raw signs rise, connect, get judged against reality, or rest in incubation.

01 · Dawn (First Light) Prospective Memory

Dawn Chorus

In Roman augury, the oscines were the birds that gave signs by their song at daybreak. In augur, the Archive does not wait passively to be queried: before the analyst begins The Reading at Stage 1, the Dawn Chorus fires automatically on cue.

VOICE 1 · ALMANAC CYCLICALS: Flags unlisted recurring drops (e.g., Halifax HPI on the first Friday; FCA Dear CEO letters in January).
VOICE 2 · OPEN WINDOWS: Surfaces frozen predictions in the Prediction Ledger entering or closing their resolution window today.
VOICE 3 · RUNNING THREADS: Orientes the analyst on active multi-stage cascades mid-flight from prior editions.
VOICE 4 · THE NIGHT'S HARVEST: Presents experimental premonitions woken from DreamState, waiting at the Firebreak.
02 · Midday (The Reading) Salience & Association

Scale & Resonance

As the daytime write path shreds an approved briefing into the Signal Ledger and Station 4 classifies each story at temperature 0, two acoustic laws govern how the signal enters the sky:

SCALE (WRITE-TIME WEIGHT): The encoder's thumb on the balance. Combines deterministic MentionBreadth ("Also reporting" count) and lane prominence with institutional magnitude. High-scale signals stand tall as central spires and resist decay; low-scale mentions fade quickly unless reinforced.
RESONANCE (ASSOCIATIVE RECALL): A sign means nothing alone. When a signal matches Pattern A, sympathetic Resonance plucks the neighbouring strings wired to the same frame — automatically surfacing linked lead-lag patterns and Type 3 Clash Patterns so the analyst can FlagClash immediately.
03 · Dusk (The Confessional) Reconsolidation & Decay

Retune & Fade

As the day closes and open predictions reach their resolution window, the practice enters The Scoring. Reality is allowed to vote (hit · miss · partial), triggering two housekeeping disciplines:

RETUNE (RECONSOLIDATION): A Pattern is frozen during normal flight; it becomes briefly editable only during The Scoring when reality votes miss or partial. Analysts retune its lead-time window or confidence while pinning the prior version in its lineage — learning without hindsight forgery.
FADED (ATTENTIONAL DECAY): Unreinforced detected or incubating signals that pass their edition half-life fade out into the faded state — dropping out of active Vantage and Query Librarian views (solving SharePoint's 5,000-row ceiling) while remaining permanently in stone for back-testing.
04 · Night (The Sanctuary) Incubation & Consolidation

Incubating & DreamState

By day, each Edition is a strict templum and the waking classifier refuses to guess. By night, the engine steps into the sanctuary to discover what waking rules cannot see:

INCUBATING (THE NURSERY STATE): When Station 4 meets a novel sign it has no honest enum for, it marks the row incubating rather than planting a phantom rhyme.
DREAMSTATE (NOCTURNAL CONSOLIDATION): Offline, the engine enters DreamState — walking across incubating rows and across separate Edition skies (Finance ↔ Design ↔ LBG) to merge multi-day echoes into canonical Events, listen for Dropped Beats, and cluster recurring residue into candidate premonitions.
