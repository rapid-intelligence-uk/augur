The lead · Typography

Aeonik Soft: rounded neo-grotesque typography by CoType

Abduzeedo · 12 Nov · Foundry: CoType ·

Mark Bloom KU and CoType Foundry calibrate subtle corner radii in Aeonik Soft, warming neo-grotesque typography across eight weights. Full pill-shaped rounding broke the Black weight almost immediately.

A study in restraint: the team stepped the rounding through four visible stages to a radius that “reads warm without looking cartoonish” – keeping interior junctions crisp at ninety degrees, and nudging the upper leg of ‘K’ outwards so it holds steady across weights. Eight weights from Air to Black, with italics and 17 stylistic sets.
