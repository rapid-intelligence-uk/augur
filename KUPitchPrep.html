Sponsorship Intelligence Dossier — Adobe & Canva

Prepared for James / KnownUnknown  ·  Scope: KU LIVE event + broader brand partnership  ·  Compiled 9 June 2026  · 

The strategic spine

Adobe and Canva are fighting the same war for creative mindshare — but they are buying sponsorship for opposite reasons. James should walk in with two pitches, not one.

Adobe is the anxious incumbent. It owns roughly two-thirds of the creative-software market but its stock fell ~30% in early 2026 on AI-disruption fears, and it is spending heavily to defend cultural relevance with "real" creatives. Adobe buys sponsorship to defend legitimacy and repair trust.

Canva is the insurgent. $4bn+ revenue growing 36% a year, 265m users, an IPO on the horizon — but still fighting the "is it real design?" perception among professionals. Canva buys sponsorship to buy credibility with the creative community.

KnownUnknown sits in the exact gap both brands are trying to reach: a curated collective of working creatives. The dossier is itself a demo of the partnership — if KU arrives armed with Cision-grade intelligence, the prep is the product.
