The lead · Designers KU

40 years after Andy Warhol and Keith Haring, Montreux Jazz Festival's new poster is a dream creative double act

Creative Bloq · 5 Nov · Malika Favre and Kumkum Fernando for Montreux Jazz Festival

Montreux Jazz Festival has repeated an experiment it first tried in 1986, when Keith Haring worked with Andy Warhol on its poster. The 2027 design is a collaboration between French illustrator Malika Favre and Sri Lankan sculptural artist and designer Kumkum Fernando – a maximalist divine figure part robot, part sacred altar, part jukebox.

Favre’s 2017 Montreux poster stood out for its minimalism; ten years on, she went the opposite way. The design is also being made into a made-to-order vinyl cabinet. The 61st festival runs on the Lake Geneva shoreline from 2 to 17 July 2027.

“I fell in love with the festival on my very first visit in 2017. The poster I created for that edition remains one of my personal favourites to this day. I knew it would be hard to match, so I wanted to take the opposite approach: to create a work bursting with colour, as maximalist as the first one was minimalist.”
Malika Favre
“The spark came when we noticed the resemblance between the shape of certain religious altars and that of a jukebox, both sharing the same sense of symmetry.”
Kumkum Fernando
