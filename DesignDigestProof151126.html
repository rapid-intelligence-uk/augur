After the honeymoon

AI’s worst week, and a turn to the tangible

Three days on what happens after the hype. Creative Bloq calls it the AI industry’s worst week: US voters backed limits on data centres, and Jensen Huang resigned from Nvidia. BoF traces the money from AI stocks to luxury spending. Against it, the tangible holds its ground – a crooked plate that feels more human than the algorithm, Yoto’s lo-fi audio player, Sony’s remade Walkman headphones and Leica’s revived hammertone finish.
