The Research

Most brands are only reading
half the signal

Cision's own CMO, Amy Jones, published research in June 2026 that makes the case for exactly what a KU designer needs in a pitch room. The study analysed thousands of consumer purchase queries and found a structural difference between how people search and how they talk on social a gap that most brands have never measured.

"Brands can miss important parts of the consumer journey when they look at search intelligence and social intelligence in isolation. Search can show what people want to know. Social can show the context, emotion, and validation behind the decision."

— Amy Jones, CMO, Cision · The Question Gap Research, June 2026
Search queries
5.4
words on average
Fact-finding, transactional. "What is X?" the private, unfiltered signal of what a brand's audience actually wants. Invisible to the brand unless they're reading search intelligence. This is what Trajaan surfaces.
Social media questions
113.8
words on average
Context-seeking, emotional, evaluative. "Should I do X? Has anyone else done X?" the social validation layer. Tells you not what people want, but why they want it and whether they trust the brand to deliver. This is what Brandwatch surfaces.
01
Information-oriented
Seeking facts, specs, definitions. The brand's customers are asking "what does this product actually do?" often a signal that current communications are unclear. A designer who knows this can pitch clarity and simplicity as the creative solution.
02
Decision-validation
Seeking reassurance, social proof, peer experience. The brand's customers are not yet convinced they're looking for validation before committing. A designer who knows this can pitch brand trust and credibility as the creative priority.
03
Context-timing
Seeking "is now the right moment?" signals. The brand's customers are ready to buy but looking for a trigger. A designer who knows this can pitch urgency, seasonal relevance and campaign timing as the creative lever.

For a KU designer in a pitch room, this is transformative. They arrive knowing not just what the client's brand looks like but what the client's customers are privately thinking, what reassurance they need, and what would tip them from interested to committed. That is a creative brief hidden in data. And it comes from the combined signal of Trajaan (search) and Brandwatch (social) a combination only Cision has built.
