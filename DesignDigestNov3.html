Edition theme

By hand, on purpose

Craft as a stance, as AI floods the feed

Two days, one argument: as AI floods the feed, design is making a point of the human hand. The WSJ reports brands turning to claymation, puppets and stop-motion – e.l.f. and Levi’s among them – partly in backlash against AI imagery; Alfa Romeo’s Pascal Dadamo wants car interiors to bring back warmth against black glass and screens; and Lagos Fashion Week put craft and heritage first. The counter-current is just as busy: Very’s Christmas ad is its first to use AI-assisted production, Finasi unveils AI-designed furniture as Dubai Design Week opens, and OpenAI is renaming datacentres “compute campuses”. Mother Design’s MLB refresh leads, with a five-font family drawn from early 20th-century baseball lettering. Elsewhere, Creative Bloq asks how Airbnb’s mocked Bélo had the last laugh, Canva’s free Affinity reaches the iPad, and John Lewis’s 20th Christmas ad stars Kristin Scott Thomas.

The lead · Rebrands & New Identity · Typography

Major League Baseball’s new look pays homage to the sport’s history

Fast Company · 3 Nov · Mother Design for MLB

Major League Baseball has a new look, courtesy of an all-star lineup of type designers. Mother Design left Jerry Dior’s 1968 batter logo untouched and built a five-font family – MLB Franklin, Clarendon, Flare, Block and Script – from styles present in early 20th-century baseball.

Heritage without pastiche: a multiyear refresh that treats lettering as the sport’s inherited vernacular, then rebuilds it for retina screens and tiny spaces.

“It’s ensuring that we’ve got these visual cues and these letterforms that have been part of our visual vernacular for longer than any of us have been alive.”
Mark Sloan, New York head of design, Mother Design
“How do we make sure that we carry that forward and we honor that tradition, but that we build them in a way that is prepared for retina screens and extremely small spaces as well as emphatic, large moments.”
Mark Sloan, Mother Design
